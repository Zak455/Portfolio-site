# Hearthwild audio credits

Recorded clips (trimmed and converted to MP3) from the Sonniss #GameAudioGDC 2024 bundle, royalty-free:

- `rinse.mp3`: UberDuo, The Kitchen Sink Audio Playset
- `pop.mp3`, `lid.mp3`, `click.mp3`: Rogue Waves, Kawaii UI
- `wood.mp3`: InMotionAudio, Wood
- `branch.mp3`: Sonic Bat, Videogame Foley Essentials Vol. II

Forest, stream, wind, fire, birdsong and music are generated live in the browser (Web Audio).

# Hollowmere art credits

- **Character portraits** (`art/portraits/`) are 3D renders made with `tools/portraits3d.jsx`. Every head is built on the **"Lee Perry-Smith" head scan by [Infinite-Realities](https://ir-ltd.net)**, licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) and distributed with the three.js examples. The scan was modified: each character's head is re-sculpted (jaw, brow, cheeks, nose, age), re-skinned, given opened eyes, strand-rendered hair and facial hair, headwear, glasses and costume, then lit and colour graded.
- **Case 1 portraits** (`marsh`, `julian`, `pell`, `crane`) are illustrations supplied by the author and replace the earlier 3D renders. All other portraits are the 3D renders described above.
- **Posters, creatures and sets** are rendered from the game's own three.js sets and SVG art (`tools/keyart.jsx`).

# Audio credits

Sound effects and ambience are edits of clips from the **Sonniss "GameAudioGDC 2024" bundle**
(royalty-free, commercial use allowed, no attribution required). The raw bundle files are not
redistributed here, only short trimmed, re-encoded game-ready edits. `SOURCES.txt` lists the
source file and time range for every clip.

`dk-growl-a.mp3`, `dk-growl-b.mp3` and `dk-snarl.mp3` are reused from Hollowmere (same bundle).
Narration lives in `../voice/`.

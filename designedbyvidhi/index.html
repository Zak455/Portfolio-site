About me

Half designer,
half lawyer-in-training

I'm Vidhi, a graphic designer from Chicalim, Vasco. I make social media content, posters, flyers and brand identities, and I love turning complicated ideas into visuals that feel simple and fun.

I'm also in my 4th year of a B.A. LL.B. at V.M. Salgaocar College of Law. Law trained me to read closely, check everything twice and structure my thinking, and that's exactly how I approach layout and detail.

FAQ

Good questions.

Can you redesign the menu or sign I already have?

Yes. Send a photo of what you have now. I keep what works, fix what doesn’t, and make it easier to read.

Do you handle printing and installation?

I prepare print-ready files set up for your printer or sign maker, so they can produce it without surprises. If you don’t have one, ask me and we can talk it through.

Can you make animated menus for TV screens?

Yes. I can turn your menu or offers into a looping animation for a TV or display, and short versions for Instagram reels and WhatsApp status.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, what you need, any logo, photos or old designs, and roughly when you need it. A WhatsApp message is enough.

Do you only work in Goa?

I’m based in Madgaon and love working with local businesses, and I can also work remotely.
